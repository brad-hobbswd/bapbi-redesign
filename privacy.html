BAPBI Website Policy

Privacy Policy

How Bethesda Apostolic Pentecostal Bible Institute handles information submitted through this website.

Our Commitment to Privacy

Bethesda Apostolic Pentecostal Bible Institute, known as BAPBI, respects the privacy of individuals who visit this website, inquire about the institute, or submit an enrollment application. This policy explains what information may be collected, how it may be used, and the choices available to you.

Information We Collect

Information may be collected when you voluntarily submit an enrollment application, contact the institute, or communicate with us. This may include your name, email address, preferred name, enrollment preferences, ministry or study experience, and information included in your message.

How We Use Information

  • To review and process enrollment applications.
  • To communicate with applicants and students.
  • To provide information about classes, schedules, resources, and institute activities.
  • To maintain administrative and educational records.
  • To respond to questions, requests, and correspondence.

Google Forms and Other Services

Enrollment information is submitted through Google Forms and may be stored in associated Google services, including Google Sheets. Those services operate under their own privacy policies and security practices. BAPBI does not sell applicant information or use enrollment information for unrelated commercial advertising.

Cookies and Website Data

This website may use basic browser functions or limited technical data necessary for operation, security, and performance. BAPBI does not intentionally use this website to collect sensitive personal information through tracking technologies.

Information Protection

Reasonable administrative and technical measures are used to limit access to information submitted to BAPBI. However, no internet transmission or electronic storage system can be guaranteed to be completely secure.

Third-Party Links

This website may link to external websites, including church resources, educational services, and Google services. BAPBI is not responsible for the privacy practices or content of external websites.

Your Choices

You may contact BAPBI to ask questions about information you have submitted or to request correction of inaccurate information, subject to applicable administrative and legal requirements.

Contact

Questions about this policy may be directed to BAPBI at brad.hobbs@mercyforyou.org or 706.614.3657.
