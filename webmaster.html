ABOUT THE WEBMASTER

Serving Through Technology

My name is Bradley Hobbs, and I have been a member of Bethesda Tabernacle since March 2019. Through faithful preaching, Bible study, prayer, and fellowship, God has continually strengthened my faith and deepened my love for His Word.

I am also a student of Bethesda Apostolic Pentecostal Bible Institute, where I continue studying the Scriptures and the apostles' doctrine. The opportunity to grow in biblical knowledge while serving the ministry has been one of the greatest blessings of my life.

Professionally, I hold a Bachelor of Science in Computer Information Technology and Business Management from Union Commonwealth University and serve as an early childhood educator. My background in technology, education, and organization enables me to develop websites that are functional, accessible, and easy to navigate.

Serving as webmaster allows me to combine those experiences in support of the institute's mission. My goal is to provide a reliable online resource where students, prospective students, church members, and visitors can easily access information about the institute, its programs, and its biblical teaching.

I am grateful for the family God has blessed me with. My son, Gabriel, continually inspires me to pursue integrity, faithfulness, and purpose. I also appreciate the love and encouragement of my partner, Justin, whose support has been a constant source of strength throughout many seasons of life.

Whether serving through ministry, education, or technology, my desire is to honor Jesus Christ by using every opportunity and ability He has entrusted to me for His glory and in service to others.

THE BAPBI WEBSITE

About This Website

The Bethesda Apostolic Pentecostal Bible Institute website serves as the institute's official online presence. It was developed to provide students, prospective students, church members, and visitors with accurate information about the institute's mission, curriculum, leadership, and educational opportunities.

The website also provides access to student resources, enrollment information, announcements, academic materials, and other ministry resources. Every page is designed to present information in a clear, organized, and accessible manner that supports biblical learning and effective communication.

As the institute continues to grow, the website will continue to expand with additional educational resources, ministry information, and tools that encourage the study of God's Word and equip believers for faithful service.

SERVICE AND PURPOSE

Why I Serve

Serving as webmaster allows me to unite my experience in technology with my desire to serve the kingdom of God. I consider it a privilege to support Bethesda Apostolic Pentecostal Bible Institute by helping maintain a professional, reliable, and welcoming online presence.

My responsibilities extend beyond creating webpages. They include organizing information, maintaining current content, improving accessibility, monitoring website performance, and ensuring visitors can quickly locate the resources they need.

Every update is made with the goal of reflecting the institute's commitment to excellence, integrity, and sound biblical teaching. My prayer is that this website serves as a valuable resource for everyone seeking to grow in the knowledge of God's Word.

EDUCATION AND EXPERIENCE

Education & Professional Background

My educational background combines technology, business, and education. I earned a Bachelor of Science in Computer Information Technology and Business Management from Union Commonwealth University, graduating on May 8, 2010. My studies provided a strong foundation in web development, information systems, organizational management, and digital communication.

In April 2020, while living in London, Kentucky, I earned my preschool teaching certification and began serving in the field of early childhood education. In 2023, I relocated to Lexington, Kentucky, where I continue working as an educator while remaining actively involved in the ministries of Bethesda Tabernacle and Bethesda Apostolic Pentecostal Bible Institute.

My experience in both technology and education has shaped the way I approach every responsibility. Whether developing websites, organizing information, or supporting students in the classroom, I strive to communicate clearly, remain organized, and serve others with excellence, integrity, and compassion.

These experiences have allowed me to contribute to Bethesda Apostolic Pentecostal Bible Institute by combining technical knowledge with a heart for ministry. My goal is to ensure that the institute's online resources remain dependable, accessible, and beneficial to students, prospective students, church members, and visitors alike.

DIGITAL STEWARDSHIP

Website Stewardship

Serving as Webmaster is both a technical responsibility and a ministry of service. I oversee the planning, design, development, maintenance, and ongoing improvement of the Bethesda Apostolic Pentecostal Bible Institute website to ensure it remains accurate, dependable, and easy to use.

My responsibilities include updating website content, organizing educational resources, improving accessibility, monitoring site performance, maintaining consistent design standards, and ensuring information remains current for students, prospective students, church members, and visitors.

Every page is developed with careful attention to clarity, usability, and long term reliability. My goal is to create an online experience that reflects the excellence, integrity, and biblical foundation of Bethesda Apostolic Pentecostal Bible Institute while making information easy to find and understand.

As the institute continues to grow, I remain committed to expanding and improving the website so it continues to serve as a valuable resource for biblical education, ministry communication, and student support. Above all, I pray that every aspect of this work brings honor and glory to Jesus Christ.

FAITH AND SERVICE

Personal Testimony

Every believer has a testimony of God's grace, and mine is a continual reminder of His faithfulness, mercy, and love. Although I had known about God for much of my life, there came a time when I realized that knowing about Him was not the same as truly knowing Him through His Word.

When I began attending Bethesda Tabernacle in March 2019, my life began to change. Through the faithful preaching of God's Word, consistent Bible study, prayer, worship, and the encouragement of a church family grounded in Scripture, my understanding of the Bible grew deeper.

Today, I continue to grow through the ministries of Bethesda Apostolic Pentecostal Bible Institute, where studying the Scriptures has strengthened both my faith and my desire to serve.

Serving as Webmaster is one of the ways I am able to give back to the ministry that has invested so much in me. It is a privilege to use the skills God has given me to help make biblical education and ministry resources available to students, church members, and visitors around the world.

I thank God for His faithfulness, for the church family He has given me, and for every opportunity to serve. My prayer is that my life, my work, and this website will always honor Jesus Christ and encourage others to know Him more deeply through His Word.
