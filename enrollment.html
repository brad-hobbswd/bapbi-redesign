THE FALL SEMESTER

Prepare for the Word.

Enrollment information is maintained within the institute's administrative records to support accurate communication, student preparation, and participation throughout the Fall Semester.

The BAPBI Fall Semester course of study will focus on biblical principles of spiritual authority through the study of Under Cover: Discover Your Response to Leadership by John Bevere.

Through this Fall Semester study, students will explore biblical perspectives regarding authority, submission, obedience, spiritual maturity, and our response to God's established leadership. This course is designed to encourage personal growth, a deeper understanding of Scripture, and continued preparation for ministry development.

REGISTRATION DEADLINE September 30, 2026

Complete the Fall Semester enrollment application before the registration deadline.

STUDENT COMMUNICATION

Stay Connected

Official Student Email

Each student is issued an official class communication email in the format firstname.lastname@bapbi.net . This email serves as the official communication channel for interaction with Administration, Instructors, and the Webmaster throughout the Fall Semester.

The name used during enrollment must match the name used for all coursework and assessments.

Communication and System Use

All digital communication and materials provided by the institute during the Fall Semester are considered confidential and are intended solely for academic use within the institute.

Institutional systems are monitored by authorized personnel, including the Webmaster, to ensure proper use and maintain system security.

Misuse of the assigned email account or institutional systems, including sharing confidential information or engaging in inappropriate communication, may result in revoked access privileges and possible dismissal from the Fall Semester program.

INSTITUTIONAL INFORMATION

Accreditation

Instruction provided by Bethesda Apostolic Pentecostal Bible Institute is not presently accredited. The institute is in its founding season of instruction, and as the program develops, leadership will prayerfully pursue appropriate accreditation opportunities as the institute continues to grow.
