ABOUT BAPBI

Rooted in Truth. Preparing Believers for Purpose.

Bethesda Apostolic Pentecostal Bible Institute exists to establish believers in Apostolic truth, strengthen spiritual understanding, and prepare students to live, serve, and lead with biblical conviction.

OUR INSTITUTE

A place for believers to study the Word, strengthen their foundation, grow in spiritual maturity, and prepare for meaningful service in the Kingdom of God.

OUR MISSION

More Than Knowledge. A Life Built on Truth.

BAPBI exists to establish believers in Apostolic truth through clear biblical teaching, intentional discipleship, and sound doctrine that produces spiritual stability and maturity.

Instruction moves beyond knowledge into confident obedience, helping students live out Scripture in daily life, worship, and service within the local church.

The goal of BAPBI is to form believers who are grounded in truth, united in faith, and prepared to serve with purpose and integrity.

WHO BAPBI SERVES

01

New Converts

Learning foundational Apostolic doctrine and building a strong biblical foundation.

02

Growing Believers

Strengthening spiritual discipline, biblical understanding, and personal conviction.

03

Future Servants and Leaders

Preparing students for meaningful service, ministry, leadership, and Kingdom purpose.

OUR FOUNDATION

Apostolic Truth. Biblical Conviction.

Teaching at BAPBI is centered on the one God revealed in Jesus Christ and the biblical truth that has shaped Apostolic faith from the beginning.

01

The Name of Jesus

Understanding the one God revealed in Jesus Christ and the significance of His name throughout Scripture.

02

Apostolic Obedience

Emphasizing repentance, baptism in Jesus' name, and the infilling of the Holy Ghost according to Scripture.

03

A Life of Faith

Encouraging prayer, devotion to the Word, discernment, holy living, and faithful commitment to the body of Christ.

BAPBI

Birthed From the Local Church

OUR COVERING

Birthed From Bethesda Tabernacle.

Bethesda Apostolic Pentecostal Bible Institute is birthed out of Bethesda Tabernacle in Lexington, Kentucky.

The Institute is connected to the life and ministry of the local church, where biblical truth is taught, believers are discipled, worship is cultivated, and individuals are prepared to serve.

BAPBI exists within that same commitment to Apostolic truth, spiritual growth, faithful discipleship, and Kingdom service.

What We Are Preparing For

Truth understood. Truth lived. Truth shared.

01

Know the Word

Develop confidence in doctrine through clear biblical teaching that strengthens understanding and removes confusion.

02

Live the Word

Build consistent habits of prayer, Scripture devotion, faithfulness to the local church, and daily discipleship.

03

Serve With Purpose

Apply biblical knowledge and spiritual maturity through worship, relationships, service, ministry, and faithful participation in the body of Christ.
