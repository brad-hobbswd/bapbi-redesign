# BAPBI Redesign

## Bethesda Apostolic Pentecostal Bible Institute

**Equipping Believers • Building Leaders • Advancing the Kingdom**

The BAPBI Redesign is the official website redesign for **Bethesda Apostolic Pentecostal Bible Institute**, created to provide a modern, clear, accessible, and purposeful digital presence for the institute, its students, faculty, leadership, and prospective students.

**Live Website:**  
https://brad-hobbswd.github.io/bapbi-redesign/

---

## About BAPBI

Bethesda Apostolic Pentecostal Bible Institute exists to equip believers with sound biblical teaching, develop leaders for the local church, and advance the Kingdom of God through Apostolic truth.

The website is designed around three central purposes:

### Equipping Believers

Providing sound biblical teaching for spiritual growth and stronger walks with God.

### Building Leaders

Developing faithful leaders with knowledge, character, and Apostolic conviction.

### Advancing the Kingdom

Preparing believers to make an impact in their churches, communities, and the world.

---

## Purpose of the Redesign

The BAPBI redesign was created to make the institute's online presence:

- Clear and easy to navigate
- Professional and modern
- Mobile responsive
- Accessible to students and visitors
- Organized around the needs of prospective and current students
- Reflective of BAPBI's Apostolic identity
- Easy to maintain and expand
- Focused on biblical education, spiritual preparation, and leadership development

The redesign brings important institute information together into a structured digital experience rather than requiring visitors to search through disconnected pages.

## Design Philosophy

The redesign follows a clean institutional design approach while maintaining the spiritual identity of BAPBI.

The design emphasizes:

- Strong typography
- Clear hierarchy
- Purposeful spacing
- Responsive layouts
- Simple navigation
- Consistent page structure
- Strong calls to action
- Student-focused accessibility
- Professional presentation
- Apostolic identity

The goal is not simply to make the website visually appealing, but to make it useful.

---

## Responsive Design

The website is designed to function across:

- Desktop computers
- Laptops
- Tablets
- Mobile phones

Navigation and page content are structured to adapt to smaller screens while maintaining access to the site's primary resources.
