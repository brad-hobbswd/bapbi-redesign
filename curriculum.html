ACADEMICS

Curriculum

The BAPBI curriculum provides clear, structured instruction that helps new believers build strong biblical foundations and apply Apostolic truth in daily life.

Teaching emphasizes prayer, Scripture, discipleship, and accountability within Bethesda Tabernacle, forming believers who live what they believe.

As BAPBI continues developing a more streamlined online format for future classes, current instruction focuses on excellence in teaching through sound doctrine and media prepared by the instructor. The purpose of the curriculum is not to train ministers, but to ground students firmly in the foundational truths of the Apostolic faith.
