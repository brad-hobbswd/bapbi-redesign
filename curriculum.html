ACADEMICS

Curriculum

A structured course of study designed to establish biblical foundations, teach Apostolic doctrine, and develop faithful disciples.

BAPBI emphasizes sound biblical teaching, prayer, church life, discipleship, and accountability within Bethesda Tabernacle.

Current instruction is centered on instructor prepared teaching and media while BAPBI develops a streamlined online format for future classes. The curriculum is designed to ground students in the foundational truths of the Apostolic faith.

FOUNDATIONS

Foundations

New Life

Prayer, Scripture, church life, and discipleship habits.

Scripture

Trust, interpretation, and faithful obedience to God's Word.

The Name of Jesus

Worship, prayer, and identity centered in His name.

DOCTRINE

Doctrine

Oneness of God

One God revealed in Jesus.

Repentance

Turning to God with faith and obedience.

Baptism in Jesus' Name

Biblical practice grounded in Apostolic authority.

DISCIPLESHIP

Discipleship

Holy Ghost Life

Walking in the Spirit with endurance.

Holy Living

Daily choices shaped by Scripture.

Discernment

Wisdom, timing, and spiritual maturity.

SERVICE PATHWAYS

Service Pathways

Training supports healthy involvement in ministry and service within the local body.
