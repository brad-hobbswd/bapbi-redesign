Chapter 1 Discussion Preparation

Class Discussion: Tuesday, October 6, 2026
Preparation: Review all 10 questions and be ready to share your thoughts during class.
Discussion Questions

Chapter 1 • Pages 15 to 26

These questions are provided before our first class so you can read, reflect, and prepare for meaningful discussion. You do not need to wait until class to think through these questions. Come ready to participate.

  1. What does the phrase “Under Cover” symbolize in various contexts according to the author?
  2. How does the author relate the idea of being “Under Cover” to the Christian faith?
  3. What significant question does the author pose regarding who is under God’s cover?
  4. How does the author illustrate the consequences of disobedience using the story of Adam and Eve?
  5. What insights does the author provide about the nature of authority based on personal experiences with his son, Alexander?
  6. What point does the author make about painful truths and their significance in spiritual growth?
  7. What does the author suggest is necessary for understanding and receiving God’s truth?
  8. What is the role of rebellion in spiritual decline according to the author?
  9. How does the author encourage readers to react when confronted with uncomfortable truths?
  10. What prayer does the author include at the end of Chapter 1 and why is it significant?
Be ready for the first class:

Read the assigned pages carefully, consider each question, and be prepared to participate in the discussion. The purpose of this assignment is preparation for our conversation, not simply completion of a reading assignment.
